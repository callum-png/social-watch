## The Sacred CTA Structure (from KB)

1. **Launch Celebration Framing**: "To celebrate our launch / To celebrate, we're giving away..."
2. **The Free Tool/Resource**: Something immediately valuable and specific — not a discount, not a trial. A TOOL or DATASET.
3. **"The Right Operator" Line**: "The right operator could use this to [specific massive outcome with numbers]."
4. **Platform-Specific Triggers**:
   - X/Twitter: "Retweet and comment '[Keyword]' and I'll DM you."
   - LinkedIn: "Comment '[Keyword]' and I'll send you a link."

### KB Examples:
- Iris: "AI agent that scans your QuickBooks... The right operator could save 6 or 7 figures per year."
- Parker: "world's first Ad Health Report... The right operator could make $100,000 by copying..."
- Arcads: "scriptwriting agent behind ads that have generated over $1B in revenue."
- Icon: "Google Drive with 2000 winning ads... Ads have driven >$1.3B in revenue."
- Slash: "AI agent that scans your bank account... to find savings."
- Marpipe: "try Marpipe for Free until your first ads are live" + physical gift

### Key Patterns:
- Giveaway is ALWAYS a specific tool/resource, not the product itself
- Has a specific claim ("behind ads that generated $1B", "2000 winning ads")
- "The right operator/person could..." bridges the tool to the outcome
- Comment trigger is always a single word related to the brand

## Phase 2: Mine Inputs

Read the **brand brief** for:
- What kind of free tool would make sense for this product
- Any giveaway/competition mentioned in the transcript
- Product capabilities that could become a standalone free tool

Read the **research brief** for:
- What the target audience desperately wants (from Reddit pain)
- Industry data that could become a valuable dataset

Read the **approved body** (flow_approved.md) for:
- Features mentioned that could spin off as a free tool
- Energy level and tone to match

---

## Phase 3: Write 2 CTA Options

**Option 1: The Tool Giveaway (Primary)**
```
To celebrate [launch], we're giving away [specific free tool].
[1-2 sentences — what it does, specific and impressive]
The right operator could use this [tool/agent/dataset] to [specific outcome with numbers].
X: Retweet and comment "[Keyword]" and I'll DM you.
LinkedIn: Comment "[Keyword]" and I'll send you a link.
```

**Option 2: The Data/Resource Giveaway (Secondary)**
```
[Different angle — dataset, spreadsheet, physical gift, extended trial]
[Specific claim with numbers]
The right [operator/person] could [specific outcome].
[Platform CTAs]
```

## SLOP TO AVOID

- Generic giveaways ("a free guide", "a free consultation")
- Missing "the right operator" line
- No specific numbers in outcome
- Comment trigger word that's boring or too long
- CTA reads like a sales pitch instead of a gift
- Missing platform-specific formatting
