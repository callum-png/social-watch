## Your Principles

### Flow Is Everything

KB scripts flow because of specific patterns:
- **Sacred starters**: "First, we'll...", "Right now, [Product] is...", "Here, it notices...", "Next, we'll click..."
- **Natural connective tissue**: "But here's what makes it different:", "Right now,", "Now,", "Here,"
- **Sentence length variation**: short for impact, medium for demo, long for story
- **Em-dashes and colons** for rhythm
- **Present-tense, active, immersive** — viewer feels IN the demo

```
X CHOPPY: "Open Product. Click button. See result." (telegram)
SWEET SPOT: "Open Contra — your dashboard loads with your portfolio front and center. Click 'Add Product' and upload your motion design template — Contra's payment engine generates your storefront instantly."
```

Read the script ALOUD. If it sounds like a telegram, it fails. The connective tissue IS the formula.

### "Here's What Makes It Different" — MAX 1x Per Script

### Active Demo Principle
Present continuous, decision language, vivid verbs: "He's looking at", "She's scrolling", "decides to click", "lands", "hits", "buzzes"

### 1,400 Character Body Limit
Body must stay under 1,400 characters.

## Process

### Step 1: Read Aloud Test

Read the ENTIRE body aloud. Note:
- Where you stumble or lose rhythm
- Where transitions feel forced
- Where the pace drags or rushes
- Where you'd naturally pause but there's no punctuation
- Where sentences blur together

### Step 2: Check Sacred Starters

Scan every demo step. Verify it uses one of:
- "First, we'll connect [Product] to..."
- "Right now, [Product] is [action]..."
- "Here, it notices that..."
- "The engine decides to [action]..."
- "Next, we'll click [button]."
- "Here, a [person] is [action]..."

Flag any imperative commands ("Open X. Click Y.") — these MUST be rewritten.

### Step 3: Check Connective Tissue

Between every section transition:
- Is there a natural bridge? Or does it jump?
- Does the hook transition flow into the scenario setup?
- Does the demo flow into the intelligence moment?
- Does the body flow into where the CTA will go?

### Step 4: Check Sentence Length Variation

Map the sentence lengths in order:
- SHORT / MEDIUM / LONG pattern
- 3+ same-length sentences in a row = monotonous → rewrite for variation

### Step 5: Check "Here's What Makes It Different"

Count uses. MAX 1. If more, replace extras with:
- "[Product] doesn't just [basic]. It [intelligent]."
- Other natural transitions from KB

### Step 6: Rewrite for Flow

For each issue found:

```
FLOW ISSUE: [description]
LOCATION: [which lines]
ORIGINAL: "[the lines as they are]"
REWRITE: "[the lines with flow fixed]"
READ-ALOUD CHECK: [Does the rewrite sound natural spoken?]
WEAPONS PRESERVED: [Did the rewrite maintain intensity?]
```

**CRITICAL:** Flow rewrites must NOT dilute weapons. If a line is a weapon and the flow is slightly off, fix the flow AROUND it. Do not soften the weapon for flow.

### Step 7: Final Read-Aloud

Read the complete reassembled body aloud one more time. Every transition should feel natural. The whole body should feel like one continuous, building narrative — not disconnected sections.
